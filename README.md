# Threat Research Reports

**Real-world cyberattacks, told as stories and examined through a defender's lens.**

This repository explores how cyberattacks unfold, how adversary techniques evolve, and what security operations teams can learn from documented incidents. Each report combines an accessible narrative with evidence-based technical analysis, threat intelligence, and actionable detection and response lessons.

## What to expect

- **The story:** What happened, who was affected, and why the incident matters.
- **Attack timeline:** The sequence of events, from initial access through impact, where evidence is available.
- **Adversary tradecraft:** Techniques, tools, and meaningful changes in attacker behavior.
- **Technical analysis:** Relevant indicators of compromise (IOCs), artifacts, and MITRE ATT&CK mappings.
- **Defender's perspective:** Detection opportunities, investigation ideas, mitigations, and lessons for SOC teams.

## Research principles

1. Link to primary sources and reputable reporting wherever possible.
2. Separate confirmed facts from hypotheses, interpretations, and illustrative reconstructions.
3. Include dates, source references, and uncertainty when details remain unverified.
4. Avoid publishing sensitive personal data or unverified attribution claims.
5. Focus on practical defensive learning rather than sensationalism.

## Upcoming reports

Case studies and research articles will be added progressively. Each report will be structured so that both curious readers and security practitioners can follow the story and investigate the underlying techniques.
